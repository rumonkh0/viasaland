Standard Tourist Visa Documentation Checklist

  • Original Passport with at least 6 months validity from travel date and minimum 2 blank pages.
  • Recent 2 copies passport-size photographs on matte paper with white background (as per embassy specs).
  • Last 6 months Bank Statement showing consistent cashflow and healthy closing balance, plus Bank Solvency Certificate.
  • Professional Proof: Trade License with English translation (business owners) OR Leave Approval letter / NOC & Pay Slips (job holders).
  • Detailed day-by-day travel itinerary with hotel bookings and flight booking slips (provided by Adiba Global).
