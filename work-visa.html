Top Employment & Work Permit Destinations

🇲🇾 Malaysia (Calling Visa & Professional Pass)

Verified calling visas for manufacturing, hospitality, plantation, and IT professionals under Malaysian Immigration approval (e-VDR). Processing: 5–10 days.

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia & 🇦🇪 UAE (Work Visas)

Government-approved employment contracts, Qiwa portal registration, Dubai 2-year resident employment visas, and free-zone work permits.

🇩🇪 Germany (Opportunity Card / Chancenkarte)

New points-based job seeker visa allowing skilled graduates and tradespeople to live and work in Germany with pathways to the EU Blue Card.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom (Skilled Worker Route)

Processing for holders of Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) across healthcare, engineering, hospitality, and tech sectors with family dependants.

Checklist for Work Permit Processing

  • Valid Machine Readable / E-Passport with at least 1–2 years validity.
  • Signed Official Employment Contract / Offer Letter / Calling Visa approval from destination country's Ministry of Labor.
  • Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) verified by Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA).
  • GAMCA / Embassy-authorized Medical Fitness Report.
  • Trade or Educational Certificates attested by relevant boards and MOFA.
